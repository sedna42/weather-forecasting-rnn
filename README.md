# Multivariate Time-Series Forecasting with Recurrent Neural Networks

A machine learning project developed to investigate the application of recurrent neural networks to meteorological forecasting, focusing on 10-day temperature and relative humidity predictions for Basel, Switzerland.

The project covers data preprocessing, feature engineering, standardization and sliding-window sequence generation. Different neural network architectures, including SimpleRNN, LSTM, GRU and hybrid models, are implemented and compared in terms of predictive accuracy, convergence and overfitting.

The study also investigates the impact of increasing the training dataset from approximately 5 to 30 years of historical meteorological observations, discussing the limitations of the resulting predictions.

**Technologies:** Python, TensorFlow, Keras, Scikit-learn, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib.
